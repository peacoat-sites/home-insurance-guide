# How Home Insurance Clear Is Written

Home Insurance Clear explains homeowners insurance in plain language: what policies cover, how premiums are set, how to file a claim and how to compare insurers. This page explains, plainly, how the articles are made.

## How articles are made

Articles on Home Insurance Clear are drafted with AI writing tools. Each draft starts from public reference sources on the topic, including those listed below, and from recent news when a topic is changing.

Before an article is published, a second automated pass reviews it and removes or rewrites anything it cannot stand behind: statistics, prices or dates it is not confident are correct, studies or organizations that may be misattributed, and personal stories presented as if they happened to the writer.

Articles are not reviewed by a licensed insurance agent before publication. Use them as background for a conversation with one, not as a substitute.

The site owner audits published articles in batches, merging pages that overlap, deleting weak ones and correcting errors.

## Who writes here

The bylines on Home Insurance Clear (Carl Brooks, Diana Foster, Kevin Park, Laura Martinez, Mark Thompson) are pen names, one for each area of coverage. They are not real individuals, they hold no professional licenses, and their profile images are illustrations, not photographs of real people.

## Sources we rely on

- [National Association of Insurance Commissioners (NAIC)](https://www.naic.org/): State insurance regulation
- [Insurance Information Institute](https://www.iii.org/): Coverage and claims data
- [FEMA](https://www.fema.gov/): Flood and disaster guidance
- [Consumer Financial Protection Bureau](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/): Consumer financial protection

Where an article depends on a specific figure, rule or study, it links to or names the source so you can check it.

## Limits of this site

Your policy documents, not our articles, decide what is covered. Coverage, exclusions and prices vary by insurer and state, so read your declarations page and policy, ask your agent or insurer about anything unclear, and contact your state insurance department if a claim dispute cannot be resolved.

## Corrections

If you find an error, please report it through the [contact page](/contact/). Reports go directly to the site owner, and confirmed errors are corrected in the article.

## How the site makes money

Home Insurance Clear may earn money from display advertising and from affiliate links, including Amazon links (as an Amazon Associate, the site earns from qualifying purchases). Advertisers and affiliate partners do not see or approve articles before they are published.
